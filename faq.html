Questions

Answers for players, families and volunteers. Can’t find yours? Write to us from the contact page.

Are meals included?

Yes. All athlete meals are catered by the High Range Club and included in the registration fee. Meal times and dietary requests

Can my family come along?

Yes. Saturday has a family programme on site, and families are welcome all three days. Entry is free for families and spectators, and no pass is needed.

What happens at Athletes’ Night?

It’s the Friday social and the tournament’s main get-together, at the High Range Club from 7 pm on Friday 2 April. Theme

How cold does Munnar get in April?

Expect warm daytime temperatures (23-26 °C), cool and pleasant mornings and evenings, and occasional pre-monsoon showers. Pack a warm layer and rain protection.

Are cleats allowed on the course?

High Range Club rules on cleats and field access

What if it rains?

Any schedule changes go to team captains on the captains’ WhatsApp group. Weather and lightning policy
