who we are

Ultimate Frisbee Kochi’s flagship tournament

Every April, club teams from across India come to the High Range Club in Munnar for three days of ultimate on a golf course in the hills.

our story

From 7 teams to 32 in four editions

Parava is organised by Ultimate Frisbee Kochi. It started with 7 teams in 2024, grew to 11 in 2025 and 25 in 2026, and expects 32 teams and around 650 athletes in 2027.

new to the sport?

What is ultimate frisbee?

Two teams of seven. Score by passing the disc, without dropping it, to a teammate in the opposite end zone.

Mixed gender

Men and women play together on the same team, on the same field.

No contact

No tackling or blocking. Pace, throws and positioning decide the game.

Self-refereed

No referees. Players make their own calls under the Spirit of the Game.

Growing fast

India ranks 18th in beach and 20th in grass ultimate worldwide.
