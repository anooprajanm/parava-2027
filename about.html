who we are

KoCatchis’ flagship tournament

Every summer, club teams from across India come to the High Range Club in Munnar for three days of ultimate on a picture-perfect field in the hills, with breathtaking views and a cool climate.

From 7 teams in 2024 to a capped 24 in 2027

It started with 7 teams in 2024, grew to 11 in 2025 and 25 in 2026. For 2027 it is capped at 24 teams, and we expect more than 500 athletes.

What is ultimate frisbee?

Two teams of seven. Score by passing the disc, without dropping it, to a teammate in the opposite end zone.

Mixed gender

Men and women play together on the same team, on the same field.

No contact

No tackling or blocking. Pace, throws and positioning decide the game.

Self-refereed

No referees. Players make their own calls under the Spirit of the Game.

Growing fast

India ranks 18th in beach and 20th in grass ultimate worldwide.
