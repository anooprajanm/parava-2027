Parava 2027: 2–4 April

Three days, 32 club teams and around 650 athletes on four fields at High Range Club, Munnar.

Three days in Munnar

  1. Friday 2 April
    Day one
    • Pool play across four fields
    • Athletes’ Night, the tournament social
  2. Saturday 3 April
    Day two
    • Crossovers and quarter-finals
    • Family programme on site
  3. Sunday 4 April
    Finals day
    • Semi-finals in the morning
    • Finals around 3 pm, then awards

Athletes and families are on site from breakfast to dinner on all three days. When detailed timings go to captains

Divisions and awards

Divisions

Mixed / open / women’s divisions and team caps

Format

Pools on Friday, crossovers and quarter-finals on Saturday, semis and finals on Sunday.

Awards

Player of the Tournament (men and women) and the Spirit of the Game trophy.
