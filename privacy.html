Privacy

This site has no accounts, no tracking and no cookies of its own. Last updated 1 November 2026.

What we collect

The volunteer form does not send anything to a server. When you press send, it opens your own email app with your name, phone, email, preferred role and availability filled in. Nothing reaches us unless you send that email to paravaultimate@gmail.com.

What we use it for

We use what you send only to organise volunteers for Parava and to reply to you.

Who in the organising team can read the inbox, and how long volunteer emails are kept

Services this site relies on

  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log your IP address when you visit.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Links to Instagram take you to Instagram, where its own privacy policy applies.

Your choices

Write to paravaultimate@gmail.com to see, correct or delete anything you have sent us.
