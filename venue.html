the venue

Four fields at High Range Club

Games are played across four picture-perfect fields in the hills of Munnar, surrounded by tea estates, with breathtaking views and a cool climate.

Finding your way around

The helpdesk sits on the path to Field 4. Hydration and physio points are between Fields 1, 2 and 3.

Travel and stay

By air

Cochin International Airport (COK) is the nearest, about 110-120 km away. Take a prepaid taxi or a ride-share, or a local bus to the nearest bus terminal. By car it’s about 3.5 hours up the ghat road.

Madurai International Airport (IXM) in Tamil Nadu is the alternative, about 140-150 km and 4.5 hours by road.

By road

From Kochi, the best route is via Aluva, Perumbavoor, Kothamangalam, Neriamangalam and Adimali on NH 85. Drive yourself or book a private taxi or app cab: about 3 hours 50 minutes, depending on traffic.

KSRTC and private buses run often from the Ernakulam and Aluva bus stands. They take about 4 to 5 hours, and fares are typically ₹160 to ₹300.

By train

The nearest stations are Aluva (AWY), about 110 km away and handy for skipping central Kochi traffic, and Ernakulam Junction (ERS) or Ernakulam Town (ERN), about 125-130 km away.

Buses and 24-hour taxis leave from right outside both stations. Munnar is about 3.5 to 4 hours on.

Weather

Mornings are often misty and evenings can be cool. Pack a warm layer and rain protection.

Check the Munnar forecast on Yr in the two weeks before the tournament.
